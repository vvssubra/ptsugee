## Terminology requiring PT SUGEE review

- `EPOCAST 36`, `Rotalign Pro`, `Boralign`, and `Levalign` remain product names in both languages.
- `Chocking` is retained in English technical headings. Bahasa uses `chocking (dudukan resin)` on first mention.
- `Flange management`, `line boring`, `flange facing`, `soft foot`, and `journal turning` retain their industry terms with short Indonesian explanations where needed.
- The site will not say that PT SUGEE currently holds active ISO certification. It will describe ISO-based quality processes and continuous improvement.
- `New Millenium` follows the spelling used in the supplied company profile.

### FAQ

**English**

1. **What services does PT SUGEE provide?** We support machinery installation and overhauling, EPOCAST 36 chocking and grouting, laser alignment, in-situ machining, flange management, ship and rig repair, offshore work, fabrication, and related industrial maintenance.
2. **Where does PT SUGEE operate?** Our work is coordinated across Indonesia and Singapore for local and overseas projects.
3. **What is in-situ machining?** It is precision machining performed at the equipment's location. It can avoid major dismantling and transport while helping reduce downtime.
4. **What is EPOCAST 36 used for?** EPOCAST 36 is a resin chocking and grouting product used beneath machinery and equipment to compensate for foundation irregularities, support load transfer, and help manage vibration.
5. **How does PT SUGEE manage quality?** We use planned quality checks, inspections, corrective action, and continuous improvement across project and service activities. Certification status can be confirmed directly for projects that require specific documentation.
6. **Can I request support for an urgent shutdown or repair?** Yes. Send the equipment details, location, required schedule, and available drawings through the enquiry form or WhatsApp so our team can assess the request.

**Bahasa Indonesia**

1. **Layanan apa saja yang disediakan PT SUGEE?** Kami menyediakan instalasi dan overhaul mesin, chocking dan grouting EPOCAST 36, laser alignment, in-situ machining, flange management, perbaikan kapal dan rig, pekerjaan offshore, fabrikasi, serta pemeliharaan industri terkait.
2. **Di mana PT SUGEE beroperasi?** Pekerjaan kami dikoordinasikan di Indonesia dan Singapura untuk proyek lokal maupun internasional.
3. **Apa yang dimaksud dengan in-situ machining?** In-situ machining adalah machining presisi yang dilakukan di lokasi peralatan. Metode ini dapat menghindari pembongkaran dan transportasi besar sekaligus membantu mengurangi downtime.
4. **Untuk apa EPOCAST 36 digunakan?** EPOCAST 36 adalah produk resin untuk chocking dan grouting di bawah mesin atau peralatan guna mengompensasi ketidakrataan fondasi, mendukung transfer beban, dan membantu mengendalikan getaran.
5. **Bagaimana PT SUGEE mengelola mutu?** Kami menerapkan pemeriksaan mutu terencana, inspeksi, tindakan korektif, dan perbaikan berkelanjutan dalam setiap kegiatan proyek dan layanan. Status sertifikasi dapat dikonfirmasi langsung untuk proyek yang memerlukan dokumen tertentu.
6. **Apakah saya dapat meminta dukungan untuk shutdown atau perbaikan mendesak?** Ya. Kirimkan detail peralatan, lokasi, jadwal yang dibutuhkan, dan gambar yang tersedia melalui formulir atau WhatsApp agar tim kami dapat melakukan penilaian.
